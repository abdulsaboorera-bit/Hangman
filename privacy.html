Privacy Policy

Hangman: Letter Quest (package: com.hangman.letterquest)

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This privacy policy explains what data the mobile game Hangman: Letter Quest ("the App") handles, how it is used, and how you can request deletion of your data. The App is a single-player word game with no user accounts and no backend servers operated by the developer.

1. Data the App Does NOT Collect

  • No account or login data (no names, emails, or passwords)
  • No contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or calendar data
  • No precise location, camera, or phone state information
  • No data is uploaded to any developer-owned server

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following information on your device only:

  • Game statistics: high score, total score, games played/won/lost, coins
  • Settings: sound, vibration, and music preferences
  • The last 20 words played (drawn from the App's built-in word list)

This data never leaves your device and is not shared with anyone. You can erase it at any time using Settings → Reset Progress in the App, which deletes all of the above.

3. Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service. When ads are shown, Google may collect:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Information about your interactions with ads (views and clicks)
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, app version)

This data is collected in transit and shared with Google for advertising and measurement purposes. It is not stored on servers owned by the developer. The App requests non-personalized ads where applicable.

Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization).

4. Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. Ads shown comply with Google Play Families policy requirements configured in the AdMob dashboard.

5. Data Retention

  • Local game data: retained on your device only until you reset it via Settings or uninstall the App.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google per Google's own retention schedules. Requests about Google-held data are handled through Google's controls (see Section 6).

6. How to Request Data Deletion

You can delete your data in two ways:

  • Game data (stats, settings, word history): open the App → Settings → Reset Progress. This immediately and permanently erases all locally stored data. Uninstalling the App also removes it.
  • Advertising data held by Google: use Google's controls at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy or the "Delete your data" options in Google Ads Settings.
  • Any other data: email us at abdulsaboorera@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request — Hangman: Letter Quest" and we will delete any data associated with you within 30 days and confirm by email.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be published at this URL. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
